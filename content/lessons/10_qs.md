```{exercise} Check-in Question
:label: ex_10-1
:nonumber:
What should the iteration for the coefficient vector $\mathbf{c}(t + \Delta t)$ in time look like for Crank-Nicolson?
```

```{solution} ex_10-1
:label: sol_10-1
:nonumber:
:class: dropdown

$$\mathbf{B}\mathbf{c}(t + \Delta t) = \mathbf{A}\mathbf{c}(t)$$

Such that:

$$\mathbf{c}(t + \Delta t) = \mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{c}(t)$$

in which we solve for the eigenvalues of $\mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{A}$.

```
