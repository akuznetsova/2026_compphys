```{exercise} Check-in Question
:label: ex_12-1
:nonumber:
In the Metropolis algorithm for a Boltzman system, the acceptance probability uses the system temperature to regulate the allowable move sets. Are moves more likely to be accepted for a larger temperature or a smaller one?

```

```{solution} ex_12-1
:label: sol_12-1
:nonumber:
:class: dropdown
For an acceptance probability $\propto e^{-\Delta/T}$ for positive values of $\Delta E$, a larger temperature results in a bigger acceptance probability. There is an easier to clear threshhold: larger allowable values of $\Delta E$. 
```
