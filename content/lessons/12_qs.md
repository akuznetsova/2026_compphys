```{exercise} Check-in Question
:label: ex_12-1
:nonumber:
If we sample with a weighting function $w(x) = x^{-1/2}$, what is the probability distribution function $p(x)$ we are sampling from?
```

```{solution} ex_12-1
:label: sol_12-1
:nonumber:
:class: dropdown

$$p(x) = w(x)/\int w(x) dx = \frac{x^{-1/2}}{2 x^{1/2} |_0^1} =  \frac{1}{2} x^{-1/2} $$
```
